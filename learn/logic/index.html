Reading these

AND — output is 1 only when both inputs are 1. Think "both conditions must hold".
OR — output is 1 when at least one input is 1.
NOT — flips a single input. Shown here acting on A only.
NAND / NOR — AND and OR with the output inverted. NAND is called functionally complete: any other gate can be built from NAND gates alone, which is why chip fabrication leans on it.
XOR — output is 1 when the inputs differ. This is the gate that makes binary addition work.
XNOR — output is 1 when the inputs match. Useful for equality checks.
