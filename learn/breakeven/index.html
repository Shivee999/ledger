When does a business start making money?

Break-even analysis answers one question: how many units must you sell before you stop losing money? Change any number below and every figure recalculates, with the formula shown so you can reproduce it in an exam.

The formulas

What each term means

Contribution per unit — what each sale contributes toward covering fixed costs, after its own variable cost is paid. Selling price minus variable cost.
Break-even point — the sales level where total revenue exactly equals total cost. Profit is zero: not a loss, not a gain.
Margin of safety — how far sales can fall before you hit break-even. A thin margin means small dips push you into loss.
P/V ratio — contribution as a percentage of sales. Higher means each rupee of sales does more work covering fixed costs.
AssumptionsBreak-even analysis assumes selling price and variable cost per unit stay constant at every volume, and that costs split cleanly into fixed and variable. Real businesses get bulk discounts, step costs and price changes — so treat this as a planning model, not a prediction.
